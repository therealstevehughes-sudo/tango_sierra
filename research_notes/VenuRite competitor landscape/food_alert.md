# Food Alert — Digital Compliance Software Profile (Alert65 / Check65)

Note on context: Food Alert (foodalert.com, UK) is primarily known as a food safety/health & safety **consultancy** that provides human auditors (Environmental Health Practitioners) for on-site audits, plus a freelance/contracted-auditor delivery model. That consultancy model is the company's historical core business and is relevant context for VenuRite (the founder admires this service model), but the focus below is their **software product line**, principally **Alert65** (platform) and its mobile companion app **Check65**.

## What is Food Alert's software product called, and what modules does it include?

### Takeaway
The flagship product is **Alert65**, a cloud-based food safety and health & safety compliance management platform, with a dedicated mobile app called **Check65** used for day-to-day digital checklists and temperature logging. Food Alert also sells adjacent/allergen-specific products (NutritionPlus, NTAssure) that appear more specialized/separate from Alert65's core compliance suite.

### Cited Findings
- Alert65 is described on Food Alert's own site as a cloud-based compliance software with "customizable modules that transform businesses with smart technology that centralizes and streamlines everyday compliance" — [foodalert.com/software/](https://www.foodalert.com/software/)
- Alert65 is marketed as "Food safety management software that makes it easy to stay compliant with food hygiene" — [foodalert.com/software/](https://www.foodalert.com/software/)
- Full module/product list found on the software overview page: Alert65 (core platform), Check65 (daily due diligence app), Bluetooth Temperature Monitoring (wireless fridge/freezer tracking), HACCP Management, Risk Assessments (200+ expert-built templates), Allergen Management, Training Management, Food Complaint Handling, Accident & Incident Investigation, Supply Chain Management (supplier certification/deadlines), NutritionPlus (allergen/nutrition data), NTAssure (food information software) — [foodalert.com/software/](https://www.foodalert.com/software/)
- The dedicated Alert65 product page confirms core modules: digital/"live" HACCP plans with customizable templates, 200+ risk assessment templates, site-specific safety policies with group-wide rollout, digital checklists and temperature monitoring, allergen matrix management, training/competency tracking with certification deadline alerts, task management with a green/amber/red priority scoring system, food complaint handling with instant reporting, accident/incident logging with legal-duty prompts, case management with document/image uploads, and EHO inspection-readiness tooling — [foodalert.com/software/alert65/](https://www.foodalert.com/software/alert65/)
- HACCP Control module specifically: helps "manage all agreed procedures in the HACCP system from one digital location," includes team-based task assignment, competency tracking, risk mapping for high-risk foods/tasks, production flowcharts, and HACCP review tracking with version history — [foodalert.com/software/alert65/haccp/](https://www.foodalert.com/software/alert65/haccp/)
- Digital Checklists module (branded "Check65" within Alert65) "keeps teams on schedule with digital checklists, instant issue flagging, and smart reporting dashboards" — [foodalert.com/software/alert65/checklists/](https://www.foodalert.com/software/alert65/checklists/)
- Safety Policies module lets users "create live policies bespoke to specific business practices that can be adjusted whenever needed and distributed throughout the organization" — [foodalert.com/software/alert65/safety-policies/](https://www.foodalert.com/software/alert65/safety-policies/)
- Third-party listing (SoftwareAdvice GB) lists a broader feature set for Alert65: Activity Dashboard, Alerts/Escalation, Audit Management, Audit Trail, Compliance Management, Compliance Tracking, Customisable Reports, Data Visualisation, Document Management, Incident Management, Monitoring, Real-Time Reporting, Risk Assessment, Risk Management, Supplier Management, Task Management, Training Management — [softwareadvice.co.uk](https://www.softwareadvice.co.uk/software/439487/alert65)
- Capterra's listing (20 total features) adds Archiving & Retention and Environmental Compliance to a similar list — [capterra.com/p/172050/FACT/](https://www.capterra.com/p/172050/FACT/)

### Inferences
- "Alert65" is the umbrella platform brand; "Check65" is specifically the front-line mobile app for checklists/temperature checks/task completion, not a separate standalone product — it's positioned as the "hands" of Alert65 for shift-level staff, while Alert65 itself is the management/admin/reporting layer.
- Supplier management and EHO-inspection-readiness are treated as headline selling points, suggesting compliance/inspection outcomes (not just task completion) are the primary value proposition Food Alert leads with.

### Gaps
- No confirmation found on whether NutritionPlus/NTAssure are sold independently of Alert65 or bundled; treat as adjacent products, not verified as part of the core Alert65 suite.
- Exact CCP/critical-limit alerting mechanics (e.g., automated escalation workflows) are described only in marketing terms ("Alerts/Escalation," "instant issue flagging"); no independent technical detail found on how escalation actually works.

## How does Food Alert integrate its software with its human auditor/consultancy service?

### Takeaway
Alert65 is explicitly sold as bundled with consultancy, not as a pure self-serve SaaS product — it is packaged inside a "Compliance Membership" alongside human EHP-led audits, a dedicated consultant, and a 24/7 advice line. The software is not marketed as available on a standalone basis separate from this service wrap, based on what's publicly visible.

### Cited Findings
- Food Alert's Compliance Membership page frames the offering as "a complete annual support solution" that bundles: Alert65 software (checklists, audits, allergen tracking, HACCP documentation, incident reporting) + professional services (audits, risk assessments, HACCP development, policy creation, 24/7 advice line access) + onboarding — [foodalert.com/services/compliance-membership/](https://www.foodalert.com/services/compliance-membership/)
- Direct quote from the membership page: "Your dedicated consultant will visit you and do a one-off session with you and your team to make sure you have everything set up to suit the way you do business." — [foodalert.com/services/compliance-membership/](https://www.foodalert.com/services/compliance-membership/)
- Audits under this model are conducted by "qualified Environmental Health Practitioners" who identify non-compliance and provide "a simple digital action plan" (i.e., audit findings feed directly back into the software) — [foodalert.com/services/compliance-membership/](https://www.foodalert.com/services/compliance-membership/)
- The Alert65 product page separately confirms: consultants help build HACCP plans and tailor policies during onboarding; "24/7 access to Food Safety experts" via advice line; structured training workshops for user adoption — [foodalert.com/software/alert65/](https://www.foodalert.com/software/alert65/)
- No pricing information or self-serve signup path is published on either the software or membership pages; the only calls to action are "contact sales" / "book a demo" forms and a phone number (+44 20 7244 1900) — [foodalert.com/services/compliance-membership/](https://www.foodalert.com/services/compliance-membership/), [foodalert.com/software/alert65/](https://www.foodalert.com/software/alert65/)

### Inferences
- Food Alert's go-to-market treats software and human consultancy as inseparable — the software is effectively the delivery/reporting layer for a consultancy relationship, not a competing self-serve product. This is a meaningfully different model from pure-play SaaS competitors like FoodDocs, Forkto, or Aquaint, who sell app access directly and cheaply/self-serve.
- This bundled, high-touch, consultant-onboarded model likely explains the very high (£20,000/yr) starting price point found on third-party listings (see pricing section) — the price reflects the human service wrap, not software alone.

### Gaps
- Could not verify whether Alert65 can be purchased as software-only without the membership/consultancy wrap — no evidence either way was found; this may simply not be offered publicly, or it may exist but isn't advertised.

## Mobile app details, UX, dashboard/reporting, multi-site management

### Takeaway
Check65 is a purpose-built companion app (iOS + Android) for daily checklist/temperature-check execution, with offline support and Bluetooth probe integration; Alert65 provides the multi-site dashboard/reporting layer on top. Independent app-store reviews are sparse but include some sharply negative technical complaints (lag, login failures, data not saving).

### Cited Findings
- Check65 (App Store listing) description: mobile companion to Alert65 for completing checklists and temperature checks "from the palm of their hand," with notifications to prevent missed checklists — [apps.apple.com/check65](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/check65/id1611955849)
- Check65 features per App Store listing: daily/weekly/monthly checklists (opening/closing, cleaning, fire safety), ad-hoc checklists (visitor logs, cleaning checks), temperature capture (hot/cold/delivery), up to 10 attachments per checklist action, Bluetooth integration with ETI temperature probes, in-app notifications, offline checklist functionality, role-based task filtering — [apps.apple.com/check65](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/check65/id1611955849)
- App Store rating for Check65: 4.0/5 stars, but based on only 2 ratings — extremely thin sample — [apps.apple.com/check65](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/check65/id1611955849)
- One App Store user complaint: "Anytime i try to login at work it goes to a white screen, also clicking anything on the login screen it does that." — [apps.apple.com/check65](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/check65/id1611955849)
- A separate, more severe complaint surfaced via web search (source: Google Play, not independently re-verified by direct fetch — attempt to fetch the Play Store page failed/returned no content): "Awful, the worst food safety system ive ever used, slow, doesn't respond to user inputs, doesn't save user inputs, needs rebuilding ground up." Another reviewer reportedly said the app "is laggy, it stops for no reason, overwrites temperature even though it has been programmed not to." — reported via [Google search summary of Google Play reviews], original Play Store page not independently confirmed
- The Caterer (trade press) coverage on Check65: built "by Food Alert's technical experts," went through "three months of rigorous testing with key clients prior to launch," was "designed with client feedback at the center," launched in the App Store as of August 2022. Features described: scheduling opening/team briefings and seasonal events, task assignment by department/site, task display by status, centralized delivery checks/temperature logs/visitor details replacing paper, a "smart dashboard" with live performance insights, push notifications for issues, multi-site visibility for operators across their whole estate, and in-app training guidance during checks — [thecaterer.com/indepth/stress-free-compliance-check](https://www.thecaterer.com/indepth/stress-free-compliance-check)
- Alert65's own dashboard/reporting (per product page): real-time compliance metrics and analytics, multi-site performance comparison showing individual location compliance percentages, customizable real-time reporting, central dashboard for top-down operational visibility, role-based permissions so "users only see the information relevant to their business or job role" — [foodalert.com/software/alert65/](https://www.foodalert.com/software/alert65/)
- Access spans "mobile, tablets and desktop" — [foodalert.com/software/alert65/](https://www.foodalert.com/software/alert65/)

### Inferences
- There's a notable gap between Food Alert's own marketing narrative (rigorously tested, client feedback-driven) and the very limited but pointed independent user complaints found (login/white-screen bug, data not saving, lag) — this suggests real technical reliability issues have existed for at least some users, even though the sample size on public app stores is too small to call it a definitive trend.
- The multi-site dashboard and role-based visibility features are pitched squarely at multi-site/enterprise hospitality operators rather than single-site independents, consistent with the tiered "Independent / Multisite / Enterprise" customer segmentation found in pricing/product materials.

### Gaps
- Google Play Check65 listing could not be directly fetched (attempt returned no usable content) — Play Store rating/review count for Check65 is unverified; only the negative quotes surfaced second-hand via search-engine summarization should be treated as lower-confidence and not independently confirmed against the primary page.
- No UX walkthroughs, screenshots, or independent App Store/Play Store review threads with dates were retrievable to establish a reliable overall app-store star rating.

## Pricing model

### Takeaway
Third-party software marketplaces (Capterra, SoftwareAdvice, GetApp) consistently list Alert65 at a **flat £20,000/year** starting price with a free trial available, though this is described as a per-business/negotiated rate rather than a per-seat or per-site published price; Food Alert's own site publishes no pricing at all and drives all prospects to a sales contact form.

### Cited Findings
- GetApp: "Cost: £20,000 per year (flat rate)," free trial available — [getapp.com/alert65](https://www.getapp.com/finance-accounting-software/a/alert65/)
- SoftwareAdvice GB: "Starting Price: £20,000.00 — Flat rate with free trial available — Flexible pricing based on complexity and required bandwidth" — [softwareadvice.co.uk/alert65](https://www.softwareadvice.co.uk/software/439487/alert65)
- Capterra: "Starting Price: £20,000 per year (flat rate), Free Trial: Available" — [capterra.com/p/172050/FACT/](https://www.capterra.com/p/172050/FACT/)
- Food Alert's own Alert65 and Compliance Membership pages publish no pricing figures; both direct prospects to contact forms or a phone number for a custom quote — [foodalert.com/software/alert65/](https://www.foodalert.com/software/alert65/), [foodalert.com/services/compliance-membership/](https://www.foodalert.com/services/compliance-membership/)
- One GetApp review rated "Value for Money" at 0.0/5 despite 5.0/5 on ease of use and features — flagged by the reviewer/analysis as suggesting cost dissatisfaction despite functional satisfaction — [getapp.com/alert65](https://www.getapp.com/finance-accounting-software/a/alert65/)

### Inferences
- The consistent £20,000/year figure across three independent marketplaces (which typically source list prices directly from vendors or vendor-submitted data) suggests this is a real, if dated or indicative, starting price rather than a scraping error — but it should be treated as a "starting"/enterprise-anchored figure, not necessarily what a small independent operator would pay, since the product is explicitly tiered (Independent/Multisite/Enterprise).
- This pricing, combined with the consultancy bundling, positions Food Alert well above self-serve competitors — this is a meaningful competitive data point for VenuRite: Food Alert competes on high-touch service + compliance assurance, not on affordability or self-serve simplicity.

### Gaps
- No confirmation of what "£20,000/year" actually includes (number of sites/users, whether it includes the compliance membership consultancy wrap or software alone) — the three sources listing this figure do not specify.
- No pricing tiers/breakdown for "Independent" (1-2 site) customers was found; it's unclear if very small operators pay dramatically less or are steered to a different (cheaper) offering entirely.

## Reviews and customer sentiment (Trustpilot, Reviews.io, Capterra, G2, App Store)

### Takeaway
Available third-party review volume for Food Alert specifically as a *software* vendor is thin and skews toward reviews of the overall consultancy relationship (auditors, staff, phone support) rather than the software UX itself; where the software is reviewed, sentiment is generally positive on usability/features but sample sizes are too small (often single-digit reviews) to be statistically meaningful, and the harsher, more detailed complaints found were about the Check65 mobile app specifically (reliability/performance), not the Alert65 platform.

### Cited Findings

- Reviews.io quotes: "Convenient notification system. Just brilliant" (Brandon Anderson); "Sophie was very helpful during her first visit since we moved food alert/alert 65" (Jeremy); "The support is fantastic, whether it be an audit, phone call or email" (Michael Waite) — [reviews.io/company-reviews/store/food-alert](https://www.reviews.io/company-reviews/store/food-alert)
- Reviews.io recurring positive themes: staff/auditor expertise and professionalism (named auditors praised: Sophie, Sapphira, John, Robbie), hands-on training/coaching on the platform, responsive phone/email support, ease of use and reporting, and increased compliance confidence — [reviews.io/company-reviews/store/food-alert](https://www.reviews.io/company-reviews/store/food-alert)
- Reviews.io: no significant negative feedback found in the sample reviewed; a few recent unverified reviews were off-topic (mentioning unrelated cybersecurity services), suggesting some review-spam/noise on the platform — [reviews.io/company-reviews/store/food-alert](https://www.reviews.io/company-reviews/store/food-alert)
- GetApp: overall 5.0/5 but based on only 1 verified review; feature ratings (document management, risk assessment, audit trail, compliance management/tracking all 5.0/5) but "Value for Money: 0.0/5" and Incident Management rated 3.0/5 — [getapp.com/alert65](https://www.getapp.com/finance-accounting-software/a/alert65/)
- SoftwareAdvice GB: overall 5.0/5 from 1 review; quote from "Kevin, Food & Beverages sector (Jan 2024)": "Really happy to have Alert 65 as part of out [sic] Compliance management tools," calling it "easy to use, helpful tool that allow access at all levels"; noted weakness: "Some accident reporting processes contain unnecessary steps" — [softwareadvice.co.uk/alert65](https://www.softwareadvice.co.uk/software/439487/alert65)

### Inferences
- The pattern across Capterra, GetApp, SoftwareAdvice, and G2 (all showing just 1 verified review each, several flagged as vendor-referred/incentivized) indicates Food Alert has very low organic engagement on independent B2B software review sites — likely because its sales motion is relationship/sales-call-driven (see pricing/bundling sections) rather than self-serve trial-driven, so buyers aren't habituated to leaving software-marketplace reviews the way SaaS-only competitors' users are.
- The consultancy-side reviews (Trustpilot/Reviews.io) are strongly positive but are largely about people (named auditors, phone support) rather than the software's UX — meaning "reviews of Food Alert" and "reviews of Food Alert's software" are two different, only partially overlapping datasets, and the latter is thin.
- The Check65 mobile app appears to be the software component most likely to draw sharp, specific technical criticism (reliability/performance issues), even though it's also the component clients interact with daily — a potential competitive opening for VenuRite if its own app experience is demonstrably more reliable.

### Gaps
- Trustpilot page itself (trustpilot.com/review/www.foodalert.com) was found via search but not directly fetched for its own rating/review count/quotes — only Reviews.io was fetched directly; Trustpilot-specific figures are not independently confirmed in these notes.
- G2's actual review page content could not be retrieved (blocked); only a search-snippet secondhand summary is available — full G2 star rating, review count, and pros/cons list are unverified.
- No case studies or named customer testimonials specifically about the *software* (as opposed to audit/consultancy service) were found on foodalert.com in this pass — worth a follow-up fetch of foodalert.com's case-study or testimonials section if a dedicated page exists, as it wasn't surfaced by the queries run.
- Overall independent software-review sample size across all platforms combined is roughly 3-5 verified reviews plus ~70 general service reviews and a couple of app-store ratings — too small to draw confident conclusions about typical customer sentiment toward the software specifically; this scarcity itself is a notable finding, not just a limitation.

## Target customer

### Takeaway
Food Alert positions Alert65 across a broad hospitality-and-beyond customer base, from independent single/dual-site operators up to national and global multi-site chains, plus adjacent regulated sectors (healthcare, education, care homes, leisure).

### Cited Findings
- Food Alert states it serves "over 6,000 hospitality businesses, from local cafés and bars to national and global restaurant and hotel chains" — [Google search summary of foodalert.com/software/](https://www.foodalert.com/software/)
- Alert65 product page confirms three explicit customer tiers: "Independent" (1-2 locations), "Multisite," and "Enterprise" (complex, large-scale operations) — [foodalert.com/software/alert65/](https://www.foodalert.com/software/alert65/)
- Industries served per the same page: healthcare, education, hospitality, care homes, and leisure venues — [foodalert.com/software/alert65/](https://www.foodalert.com/software/alert65/)
- Software overview page lists target sectors: education, healthcare, leisure and events, care homes, hotels, independent restaurants/cafés, multi-site hospitality chains, food manufacturers and suppliers, catering operations — [foodalert.com/software/](https://www.foodalert.com/software/)
- Self-reported statistics from the Alert65 page: over 3,000 businesses use the platform, 1 million safety checklists completed monthly, 95% of clients believe they're less likely to suffer safety incidents, 85% confident in achieving a 5 food hygiene rating (all marketing claims from Food Alert's own site, not independently verified) — [foodalert.com/software/alert65/](https://www.foodalert.com/software/alert65/)

### Inferences
- There's an inconsistency between "6,000 hospitality businesses" (broader company-wide figure, possibly including consultancy-only clients) and "3,000 businesses use the platform" (Alert65-specific) — suggesting roughly half of Food Alert's overall client base uses the software product, while the rest may be consultancy/audit-only clients. This split is not explicitly confirmed by Food Alert and is an inference.
- The stated £20,000/year price point combined with the "Enterprise" tier language suggests Food Alert's software sales motion is weighted toward larger multi-site/enterprise accounts even though marketing language nominally includes independents.

### Gaps
- No independently verified customer count (all figures are self-reported by Food Alert, not corroborated by third parties).
- No named case studies with quotable operator testimonials specifically about software ROI/outcomes were retrieved in this research pass.
