# VenuRite Client Agreement

**Draft version 2026-10-17**

> **⚠️ Draft for solicitor review. This is not legal advice.**
> - Prepared as a starting point, to be checked by a qualified solicitor in England and Wales before use.
> - Notes marked **[Solicitor]** flag points that need specific attention.
> - This agreement is meant to be accepted online at sign-up ("I agree", with the time and version recorded). It sits alongside the Terms of Service (for individual users) and the Data Processing Agreement (Schedule 2).

---

## 1. The parties

1.1 This agreement is between **VenuRite Ltd**, a company registered in England and Wales ("**VenuRite**", "**we**"), and the business that signs up ("**the Client**", "**you**").

1.2 The person accepting this agreement confirms they are authorised to bind the Client.

## 2. What we provide

2.1 **The service.** VenuRite provides the VenuRite software (the "**Service**"):
- the web app, the Android app and any future apps;
- task checklists, training guides and quizzes;
- reports and the audit export;
- the AI assistant;
- any add-ons the Client turns on, such as the Rota add-on.

2.2 We will provide the Service with reasonable skill and care, and keep it reasonably available. We aim for availability above 99% in each month, excluding planned maintenance, which we will try to do outside UK trading hours. **[Solicitor]** Decide whether to make this a contractual service level with service credits, or keep it as an aim.

2.3 We may improve and change the Service. We won't remove a core feature the Client relies on without at least 30 days' notice.

## 3. What VenuRite is, and isn't

3.1 VenuRite is a **record-keeping and workflow tool**. It helps the Client record and manage its own food safety and workplace compliance.

3.2 It is **not**:
- a substitute for the Client's own legal duties;
- professional advice;
- a guarantee of passing any inspection, audit or hygiene rating.

3.3 **The Client stays responsible** for:
- the safety of its food, premises and operations;
- training and supervising its staff;
- the accuracy of what its staff record;
- acting on what the Service shows, such as failed checks and overdue tasks.

3.4 **Standard content.** Task templates, training guides, quiz questions and temperature limits are general guidance based on UK practice. The Client must check they suit its own premises, equipment and processes, and adapt them where needed. The Service lets managers edit guides for their venue.

3.5 **AI features.** These include the assistant and AI-drafted quiz questions or documents.
- AI output is generated automatically. It may be incomplete or wrong.
- It is guidance only, not advice.
- AI-drafted content is only used once a Client manager has reviewed and approved it. The Client is responsible for what it approves.

3.6 **Integrity flags.** The Service can flag patterns that suggest a check wasn't really done, such as checks submitted too fast or the same photo used twice. These flags:
- are aids for the Client's managers;
- are not proof of wrongdoing;
- won't catch every false record.

The Client decides what, if anything, to do about a flag, and must treat its staff fairly when doing so.

## 4. The Client's responsibilities

4.1 The Client will:
- give accurate account, venue and staff information;
- make sure staff use the Service honestly and accurately, and keep PINs, passwords and venue devices secure;
- tell staff about any monitoring features it turns on (photo checks, location checks at clock-in, integrity flags). It will use the staff notices the Service provides, or its own;
- have a lawful basis for the staff data it puts into the Service, and comply with data protection law as the controller of that data (see Schedule 2);
- keep a fallback, such as paper records, for any time the Service is unavailable;
- not misuse the Service. That means no attempts to access other clients' data, reverse engineer it, overload it, or resell it without our agreement.

4.2 **Client staff.** The Client is responsible for the acts and omissions of its staff and anyone else it lets use the Service, as if they were the Client's own. **[Solicitor]** This is the key protection the founder asked for: client-staff error stays the client's responsibility.

## 5. Fees and payment

5.1 **Fees.** Fees are charged per branch per month, by staff-size band, at the prices on the Client's billing page when it subscribes:
- **Bands:** Bistro, Brasserie, Banquet, Grand, Empire.
- **Band size:** set by the number of staff who used the Service at that branch in the month.
- **Head office:** businesses with 4 or more branches also pay a head-office charge.
- **Add-ons:** charged as shown, for example the Rota add-on.

5.2 **Yearly option.** Paying yearly costs 11 months' fees for 12 months. Yearly fees are paid in advance.

5.3 **Band changes.** A branch moves band automatically when its active staff count changes. The new price applies from the next billing month, and we will show it on the billing page.

5.4 **Payment.** Payment is by Direct Debit through GoCardless, on the date shown. All prices exclude VAT, which is added at the current rate.

5.5 **Price increases.** We may increase prices once a year, with at least 30 days' notice. If the Client doesn't accept the increase, it may cancel before it takes effect.

5.6 **Discounts.** Discount codes apply on the terms shown when they are redeemed: for example, 50% for a year, or for life of the subscription. They can't be exchanged for cash.

5.7 **Late payment.** If a payment fails and isn't paid within 14 days of us telling the Client, we may suspend the Service, after giving 7 days' further notice. Records stay safe while the Service is suspended.

## 6. Free trial

6.1 Any free trial lasts for the period shown at sign-up. No fees are charged during it. At the end, the subscription continues only if the Client has set up payment.

## 7. Term and cancellation

7.1 **Monthly plans** run month to month. Either party can cancel with 30 days' notice, ending at the end of a billing month.

7.2 **Yearly plans** run for 12 months and then renew for another year, unless either party gives 30 days' notice before renewal. Yearly fees are not refundable for unused months, except under clauses 5.5, 7.3 or 10.4.

7.3 **Ending for breach.** Either party may end this agreement straight away by notice if the other:
- seriously breaches it and doesn't put that right within 30 days of being asked to; or
- becomes insolvent.

7.4 **When the agreement ends:**
- the Client can export its records (audit export and data export) for **30 days**;
- after that, we delete the Client's data within a further 60 days, apart from anything we must keep by law, such as invoices;
- backups are overwritten in their normal cycle.

**[Solicitor]** Check these periods against the Client's own record-keeping needs. Food-safety records are often kept for longer, and the Client should export them.

## 8. Data protection

8.1 For the personal data the Client puts into the Service:
- **the Client is the controller and VenuRite is the processor.** The Data Processing Agreement in **Schedule 2** forms part of this agreement;
- for our own account and billing contacts, VenuRite is a controller, under our Privacy Policy.

8.2 We may use anonymised, aggregated information to improve the Service, for example how long checks take on average. This never identifies the Client, its venues or its staff.

## 9. Intellectual property

9.1 We own the Service, including the standard templates, guides and quiz questions. The Client gets a non-exclusive, non-transferable right to use the Service during this agreement.

9.2 The Client owns its records and the content it creates, such as its own guides, tasks and photos. The Client gives us permission to host and process that content only to provide the Service.

## 10. Liability

10.1 **Nothing in this agreement limits liability for:**
- death or personal injury caused by negligence;
- fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation;
- anything else that can't legally be limited.

10.2 Subject to clause 10.1, **we are not liable for:**
- **(a) indirect losses:** loss of profit, revenue, business, goodwill or reputation, or any indirect or consequential loss;
- **(b) the Client's own failures:** losses from the Client's (or its staff's) food safety, health and safety or legal compliance. That includes food incidents, illness, inspections, ratings, fines, prosecutions, licence action and claims by the Client's customers or staff, unless they were caused by our breach of this agreement;
- **(c) the Client's choices:** losses from the Client relying on standard content or AI output without checking it, as clause 3 requires;
- **(d) inaccurate records:** losses from inaccurate records entered by the Client's staff.

10.3 Subject to clause 10.1, **our total liability** in any 12-month period, however it arises, is limited to the fees the Client paid in the 12 months before the event giving rise to the claim. **[Solicitor]** Consider a separate, higher cap for data-protection breaches. Customers may ask for one.

10.4 If the Service is materially unavailable for more than 5 working days in a row through our fault, the Client may cancel and receive a refund of any prepaid fees for the remaining period.

10.5 **[Solicitor]** Clauses 10.2–10.3 must be reasonable under the Unfair Contract Terms Act 1977. Please check they would survive a reasonableness challenge, given that our clients are mostly small businesses.

## 11. Indemnity

11.1 The Client will indemnify VenuRite against third-party claims, and reasonable costs, arising from:
- the Client's or its staff's food safety, health and safety or legal compliance;
- inaccurate or falsified records;
- the Client's breach of data protection law as controller;
- the Client's misuse of the Service.

11.2 We will tell the Client promptly about any such claim, let the Client handle it, and not settle it without the Client's agreement. **[Solicitor]** Check whether this indemnity is proportionate and likely to be accepted by larger customers.

## 12. Confidentiality

12.1 Each party keeps the other's confidential information confidential and uses it only for this agreement. This doesn't apply to information that is public, already known, independently developed, or required to be disclosed by law.

## 13. General

13.1 **Changes.** We may update this agreement. For material changes we will give at least 30 days' notice and ask for acceptance in the app. A Client that doesn't accept may cancel before the change takes effect.

13.2 **Force majeure.** Neither party is liable for delays caused by events beyond its reasonable control.

13.3 **Transfer.** We may transfer this agreement to a buyer of our business, with notice. The Client may transfer it only with our written agreement.

13.4 **Entire agreement.** This agreement, its schedules, and the Terms of Service for individual users are the whole agreement between the parties.

13.5 **No third-party rights.** No one else has rights under this agreement under the Contracts (Rights of Third Parties) Act 1999.

13.6 **Governing law.** This agreement is governed by the law of England and Wales, and its courts have exclusive jurisdiction. **[Solicitor]** Review this for Scottish and Northern Irish clients.
