## Check Fridge Temperature
? What is the highest reading that passes for a fridge?
+ 8°C
- 10°C
- 12°C
? What should you aim to keep a fridge at?
+ 5°C or below
- 8°C to 10°C
- 0°C or below
? Where should you take the reading from?
+ The display, or a probe in a sample item
- The air right next to the open door
- The outside of the fridge door
? Why does a fridge above 8°C matter?
+ Bacteria grow fast and it breaks the law for most chilled food
- The food will start to freeze and lose its texture
- It only changes how the food looks on the plate
? The fridge reads 11°C. What do you do first?
+ Re-check in 15-20 minutes; move food if still high
- Turn the dial to its coldest and record a pass
- Leave it, as long as the food still smells fine
? Chilled food has been above 8°C for over 4 hours. What happens to it?
+ It must be thrown away
- It can be served if it is cooked well
- It goes back in the fridge and is used first

## Freezer temperature
? What reading passes for a freezer?
+ -18°C or colder
- -10°C or colder
- 0°C or colder
? Apart from the reading, what else should you check?
+ Food is hard frozen with no sign of thawing
- The freezer is packed full to the very top
- The light comes on when the door opens
? Why is -18°C the target?
+ At -18°C bacteria stop growing
- At -18°C all bacteria are killed
- It is the coldest any freezer can go
? Why can food that thawed and was refrozen be a problem?
+ It can be unsafe to eat
- It always tastes better the second time
- It is only a problem for ice cream
? The freezer has failed and food is soft. What do you do?
+ Move it; use soft food now if safe, or bin it; tell your manager
- Turn the freezer to its coldest setting and refreeze it all
- Leave the food where it is and just record the reading
