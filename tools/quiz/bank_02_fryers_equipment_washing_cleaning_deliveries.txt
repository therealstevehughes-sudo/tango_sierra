## Oil temperature
? What should you do before reading the oil temperature?
+ Let the fryer heat up fully
- Switch the fryer off first
- Add fresh oil to the vat
? With standard settings, what is the highest oil temperature that passes?
+ 185°C
- 200°C
- 220°C
? What does an amber reading above 180°C mean?
+ It passes, but turn the thermostat down
- It fails and the oil must be changed
- Nothing; amber can be ignored
? How can you read the oil temperature?
+ The thermostat, or a suitable thermometer
- Drop in a chip and count the bubbles
- Hold your hand above the oil surface
? Why does oil that runs too hot matter?
+ It breaks down faster, with harmful compounds and fire risk
- It cooks the food too slowly and leaves it pale and greasy
- It only changes the colour of the batter
? The oil is too hot and the thermostat will not control it. What do you do?
+ Switch off and report it
- Add cold oil to cool it
- Keep frying, but faster

+ Change it and record it in the oil change log
- Top it up with fresh oil and keep using it
- Strain it through a cloth and carry on
