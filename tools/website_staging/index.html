Questions

Good to know

Does this replace my EHO inspections?

No - VenuRite is a tool that helps you keep honest, complete records and stay on top of your own compliance day to day. It doesn't replace your legal responsibilities or your Environmental Health Officer's visits, but it does mean you're never scrambling to pull records together when they come.

Do my staff need training to use it?

It's built to be used with a PIN and a tap - most staff need no more than a minute shown to them on their first shift. No accounts to remember, no logging in and out all day. And every task comes with a short step-by-step guide, so nobody has to guess how to do a check properly. Managers can tailor each guide to match how your venue does things.

What happens if we lose internet connection?

Your team keeps working. VenuRite is built so a patchy kitchen connection never stops someone logging a check - it never gets in the way of the job actually getting done.

Can I try it before committing?

Yes - every account starts with a 14-day free trial, no card required. Get in touch and we'll get you set up.
