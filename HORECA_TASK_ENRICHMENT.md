# HORECA_TASK_ENRICHMENT.md — Job-Role Tags + Guidance Text (DRAFT for review)

Adds two things to every task in HORECA_TASK_LIBRARY.md:
- **Role**: which job-role it defaults to (Chef/Cook · Kitchen Porter (KP) · FOH · Bar · Management). Defaults, not lockouts — a manager can still assign anything to anyone.
- **Guidance**: short "what to do / what to record" shown to the worker on the task screen, so nobody guesses.

**Review note:** guidance is written to be clear to a real worker. Anything with a number still needs professional sign-off (same caveat as the limits). Where a task could reasonably be more than one role, I picked the most common and noted it — flag any you'd move.

Format: **Task** — Role — Guidance

---

## SEGMENT 1 — FOOD SAFETY & TEMPERATURE CONTROL

### 1.1 Refrigeration temperatures
- **Fridge temperature** — Chef/Cook — Read the fridge's display or probe an item. Record the number. Photo the display. Flag if above 8°C.
- **Freezer temperature** — Chef/Cook — Read the display, record the number, photo it. Flag if warmer than -18°C.
- **Walk-in cold room temperature** — Chef/Cook — Read the wall thermometer, record it, photo it. Flag if above 8°C.
- **Blast chiller cycle temperature** — Chef/Cook — Record start and end temperature of the cycle. Food should go from 70°C to below 3°C within 90 minutes.
- **Display/serve-over fridge temperature** — Chef/Cook — Check the chilled display holding food for service. Record the reading, photo it. Must stay at or below 8°C.

### 1.2 Cooking, reheating & cooling
- **Cooked food core temperature** — Chef/Cook — Probe the thickest part of the food. Record the number, photo it. Must reach 70°C for 2 min (or 75°C).
- **Reheated food core temperature** — Chef/Cook — Probe the centre of the reheated item. Record it, photo it. Must be piping hot throughout (~70°C; Scotland 82°C).
- **Hot-holding temperature** — Chef/Cook — Probe food held hot for service. Record it, photo it. Must stay at or above 63°C.
- **Cooling log (cooked→chilled)** — Chef/Cook — Note the time cooking finished and the time it reached fridge-cold. Must be below 8°C within 90 minutes.
- **Reheat-once verification** — Chef/Cook — Confirm this item hasn't been reheated before. Food may only be reheated once, then discarded.
- **Probe calibration check** — Chef/Cook — Test the probe in iced water (should read ~0°C) and boiling water (~100°C). Record both. Flag if off by more than 1°C.
- **Probe sanitised between uses** — Chef/Cook — Wipe the probe with a sanitiser wipe before and after each use.

### 1.3 Date marking & rotation
- **Use-by / best-before date check** — Chef/Cook — Check opened and stored items for their dates. Note and remove anything past its use-by date.
- **FIFO stock rotation** — Chef/Cook — Confirm older stock is in front / used first. Move newer deliveries behind existing stock.
- **Opened-product date labelling** — Chef/Cook — Confirm opened items are labelled with the date opened. Label any that aren't.
- **Controlled defrost log** — Chef/Cook — Confirm items are defrosting in the fridge (below 8°C), not at room temperature. Note what's defrosting.

## SEGMENT 2 — ALLERGEN MANAGEMENT
- **Allergen matrix current & accessible** — Management — Confirm the allergen chart matches the current menu and staff can find it. Update if the menu changed.
- **Allergen review on new/changed dishes** — Management — For any new or changed dish, record which of the 14 allergens it contains.
- **PPDS labelling correct (Natasha's Law)** — Management — Check pre-packed-for-direct-sale items have a full ingredient list with allergens emphasised. Photo a sample label.
- **Separate allergen prep area/equipment** — Chef/Cook — Confirm allergen-free orders are prepped with clean, separate equipment and surfaces.
- **Allergen-free order verified end-to-end** — Chef/Cook — Confirm the specific allergen-free order was kept separate from prep to plate. Note the order.
- **Purple allergen boards/cloths used** — Chef/Cook — Confirm the purple (allergen) boards and cloths are used for allergen-free prep.
- **Staff allergen briefing** — Management — Confirm staff on shift have been briefed on today's allergen info.

## SEGMENT 3 — PERSONAL HYGIENE & PPE
- **Handwashing on entry / between tasks** — Kitchen Porter (KP) — Confirm hands washed on entering the kitchen and between tasks. (All roles — tag as everyone.)
- **Clean uniform / apron** — Chef/Cook — Confirm clean uniform/apron worn at start of shift.
- **Hair covering / beard net** — Chef/Cook — Confirm hair covering / beard net worn.
- **No jewellery / false nails** — Chef/Cook — Confirm no jewellery, false nails, or nail varnish.
- **Fitness-to-work / illness declaration** — Management — Confirm each worker is fit to work and free of sickness/diarrhoea (48-hour rule). Note any exclusions.
- **Cuts covered (blue plaster)** — Chef/Cook — Confirm any cuts/grazes are covered with a blue detectable plaster.
- **Gloves available & changed appropriately** — Chef/Cook — Confirm gloves are stocked and changed between different tasks.

## SEGMENT 4 — REFRIGERATION & COLD STORAGE (equipment condition)
- **Fridge door seal intact** — Chef/Cook — Check the rubber seal isn't torn or loose. Note any damage.
- **Freezer ice build-up check** — Kitchen Porter (KP) — Check for excessive ice. Note if it needs defrosting.
- **Walk-in shelving clean & sound** — Kitchen Porter (KP) — Confirm shelves are clean and not rusted/damaged.
- **Condenser / vents dust-free** — Kitchen Porter (KP) — Check the vents/grille are clear of dust. Photo. Clean if needed.
- **Fridge/freezer alarm functioning** — Management — Confirm the temperature alarm works.
- **Not overloaded (airflow)** — Chef/Cook — Confirm units aren't so packed that air can't circulate.

## SEGMENT 5 — COOKING LINE EQUIPMENT
### 5.1 Fryer & oil
- **Oil temperature** — Chef/Cook — Read the fryer's set temperature, record it, photo it. Typically at or below 180°C.
- **Oil quality (TPM/colour)** — Chef/Cook — Check oil colour/smell or TPM reading. Record it. Discard if dark/foul or over the meter's limit.
- **Oil filtering / polishing** — Chef/Cook — Confirm oil filtered/polished as scheduled.
- **Oil change & log** — Chef/Cook — Record when oil was changed and in which fryer.
- **Fryer deep clean** — Kitchen Porter (KP) — Drain, clean, and confirm the fryer is degreased. Photo.
- **Oil usage log** — Chef/Cook — Record oil added/used today.

### 5.2 Ovens / grills / hobs / other
- **Oven working temperature** — Chef/Cook — Confirm the oven reaches and holds its set temperature. Record.
- **Combi self-clean run** — Kitchen Porter (KP) — Confirm the combi's self-clean cycle was run.
- **Grill / salamander clean & working** — Chef/Cook — Confirm clean and heating properly.
- **Hob / burner ignition & flame** — Chef/Cook — Confirm all burners light and burn with a clean blue flame. Flag any that don't.
- **Rotisserie / kebab machine temp & clean** — Chef/Cook — Confirm at temperature and clean. Record.
- **Steamer descale** — Kitchen Porter (KP) — Confirm descaled as scheduled.
- **Extraction canopy filters clean** — Kitchen Porter (KP) — Check the extraction filters are grease-free. Photo. Grease build-up is a fire risk.

### 5.3 Mechanical & safety
- **Gas interlock / emergency cut-off test** — Management — Confirm the gas interlock/emergency cut-off works.
- **Equipment guard / cut-out intact** — Chef/Cook — Confirm safety guards and cut-outs are in place and working.

## SEGMENT 6 — WASH-UP / DISHWASH
- **Dishwasher wash temperature** — Kitchen Porter (KP) — Read the wash-cycle temperature, record it. Should be ~55-65°C.
- **Dishwasher rinse temperature** — Kitchen Porter (KP) — Read the rinse temperature, record it. Should reach ~82°C to sanitise.
- **Detergent / rinse-aid levels** — Kitchen Porter (KP) — Check detergent and rinse-aid aren't empty. Refill if low.
- **Dishwasher filter cleaned** — Kitchen Porter (KP) — Remove and clean the filter. Confirm done.
- **Glasswasher functioning & dosed** — Bar — Confirm the glasswasher runs and is dosed with detergent.
- **Pot-wash sanitiser strength** — Kitchen Porter (KP) — Confirm the sanitiser sink is mixed to the right strength. Record.
- **Air-dry (no tea-towel drying)** — Kitchen Porter (KP) — Confirm items are left to air-dry, not dried with cloths.

## SEGMENT 7 — CLEANING & SANITATION
### 7.1 Food-contact surfaces & equipment
- **Prep surfaces cleaned & sanitised** — Chef/Cook — Clean then sanitise prep surfaces. Confirm the sanitiser's contact time was left before wiping.
- **Chopping boards colour-coded & sound** — Chef/Cook — Confirm the right colour board is used for each food type and none are deeply scored.
- **Slicer / mincer strip-down clean** — Kitchen Porter (KP) — Strip down, clean, and sanitise the slicer/mincer. Photo. Mind the blade.
- **Can opener blade clean** — Kitchen Porter (KP) — Confirm the blade is clean and rust-free.
- **Ice machine clean & descaled** — Kitchen Porter (KP) — Confirm the ice machine is clean and descaled. Photo.

### 7.2 Floors, walls, drains
- **Kitchen floor cleaned** — Kitchen Porter (KP) — Sweep and mop. Confirm done.
- **Drains / gullies cleared** — Kitchen Porter (KP) — Clear and sanitise floor drains. Confirm no blockage/smell.
- **Walls / splashbacks wiped** — Kitchen Porter (KP) — Wipe down walls and splashbacks.
- **Bin areas cleaned** — Kitchen Porter (KP) — Clean around and under bins.

### 7.3 Schedule verification
- **Deep clean checklist** — Kitchen Porter (KP) — Work through the deep-clean checklist items and confirm each done.
- **Cleaning schedule signed off** — Management — Confirm the day's cleaning schedule is complete and sign it off (this is your EHO evidence).

## SEGMENT 8 — CLEANING CHEMICALS & CONSUMABLES
- **Sanitiser in stock & in date** — Kitchen Porter (KP) — Confirm sanitiser is available and in date.
- **Degreaser in stock** — Kitchen Porter (KP) — Confirm degreaser available.
- **Blue roll / paper towel stocked** — Kitchen Porter (KP) — Confirm blue roll and hand towels stocked.
- **COSHH sheets present & chemicals labelled** — Management — Confirm COSHH data sheets are on file and chemicals correctly labelled.
- **Chemical dilution / dosing correct** — Kitchen Porter (KP) — Confirm chemicals are mixed/dosed to the right strength. Record.

## SEGMENT 9 — DRY & AMBIENT STORAGE
- **Dry store temperature / humidity** — Chef/Cook — Record the dry store temperature/humidity.
- **Stock off floor / on shelving** — Kitchen Porter (KP) — Confirm all stock is off the floor on shelving.
- **Open dry goods sealed & dated** — Chef/Cook — Confirm opened dry goods are sealed and date-labelled.
- **No damaged / bloated / infested packaging** — Chef/Cook — Check for damaged, bloated, or pest-damaged packaging. Note and remove any.

## SEGMENT 10 — DELIVERIES & GOODS IN
- **Chilled goods temp on arrival** — Chef/Cook — Probe chilled items on delivery. Record, photo. Reject if above 8°C.
- **Frozen goods temp on arrival** — Chef/Cook — Check frozen items are solid/frozen. Record, photo. Reject if soft.
- **Vehicle / driver hygiene** — Chef/Cook — Note the delivery vehicle/driver looks clean and hygienic.
- **Packaging intact** — Chef/Cook — Confirm packaging isn't damaged or open.
- **Use-by dates acceptable** — Chef/Cook — Confirm delivered items have acceptable dates (enough shelf life).
- **Reconciled to order/invoice** — Management — Confirm what arrived matches the order/invoice. Note discrepancies.
- **Rejected items logged** — Chef/Cook — Note any items rejected and why.
- **Supplier traceability captured** — Management — Confirm delivery records kept (supplier, date) for traceability.

## SEGMENT 11 — UTILITIES & SAFETY
- **Hot water at sinks** — Kitchen Porter (KP) — Confirm hot water available at all sinks.
- **Hand-wash sinks stocked** — Kitchen Porter (KP) — Confirm soap and towels at every hand-wash sink.
- **Fire exits clear & unlocked** — Management — Confirm fire exits are clear and unlocked.
- **Fire extinguishers in place & in date** — Management — Confirm extinguishers present and in date.
- **First aid kit stocked** — Management — Confirm first aid kit stocked.
- **Lighting functional** — Kitchen Porter (KP) — Confirm work-area lighting works. Note any out.
- **Gas / electrical no visible faults** — Chef/Cook — Note any visible gas or electrical faults.

## SEGMENT 12 — WASTE & PEST CONTROL
- **General waste removed & bins clean** — Kitchen Porter (KP) — Empty bins and confirm clean.
- **Food waste segregated** — Kitchen Porter (KP) — Confirm food waste separated correctly.
- **Used cooking oil stored / collected** — Kitchen Porter (KP) — Confirm used oil stored for licensed collection.
- **Pest activity check (droppings/gnaw/nest)** — Kitchen Porter (KP) — Look for droppings, gnaw marks, nests, or flies. Note and report any signs.
- **Fly killer / bait stations working** — Kitchen Porter (KP) — Confirm fly killer/bait stations are working.
- **External bin area secure & clean** — Kitchen Porter (KP) — Confirm outside bin area is clean and lids closed.
- **Pest control contract visit log** — Management — Record the pest control contractor's visit/report.

## SEGMENT 13 — PREVENTIVE MAINTENANCE
- **Equipment fault log reviewed** — Management — Review outstanding equipment faults. Note status.
- **Scheduled servicing up to date** — Management — Confirm scheduled servicing is current.
- **PAT / electrical inspection in date** — Management — Confirm portable appliance testing is in date.
- **Extraction/duct professional clean in date** — Management — Confirm the extraction/duct deep-clean certificate is in date.
- **Gas safety certificate in date** — Management — Confirm the gas safety certificate is in date.

## SEGMENT 14 — STOCK CONTROL
- **Stock count / par levels** — Management — Count stock against par levels. Record.
- **Wastage / spoilage log** — Chef/Cook — Record any food wasted or spoiled and why.
- **Low-stock reorder flagged** — Chef/Cook — Note items running low that need reordering.
- **High-value stock reconciled** — Management — Reconcile high-value stock (e.g. meat, spirits). Record.

## SEGMENT 15 — OPENING PROCEDURES
- **Opening checklist complete** — Chef/Cook — Work through the opening checklist. Confirm each item.
- **All refrigeration temps at open** — Chef/Cook — Check and record all fridge/freezer temps at opening.
- **Equipment switched on & warmed** — Chef/Cook — Switch on and confirm cooking equipment is up to temperature.
- **No overnight pest / leak / fault** — Chef/Cook — Check for any overnight pest signs, leaks, or faults. Note any.

## SEGMENT 16 — CLOSING PROCEDURES
- **Closing checklist complete** — Chef/Cook — Work through the closing checklist. Confirm each item.
- **Equipment safely off** — Chef/Cook — Confirm all cooking equipment safely switched off.
- **Perishables stored / covered / dated** — Chef/Cook — Confirm perishables are covered, dated, and stored correctly.
- **Final clean-down** — Kitchen Porter (KP) — Confirm final clean-down of surfaces and floors done.
- **Premises secured / alarm set** — Management — Confirm premises locked and alarm set.

## SEGMENT 17 — SERVICE READINESS
- **Mise en place complete** — Chef/Cook — Confirm prep for service is complete.
- **Hot-hold / bain-marie pre-heated** — Chef/Cook — Confirm bain-marie is up to temperature (63°C+) before service. Record.
- **Specials / allergen info briefed** — Management — Confirm the team is briefed on specials and allergens.
- **Service fridges stocked & at temp** — Chef/Cook — Confirm service fridges stocked and at temperature. Record.

## SEGMENT 18 — FRONT OF HOUSE / SERVICE
- **Dining area cleaned & set** — FOH — Confirm dining area cleaned and tables set.
- **Tables / condiments sanitised** — FOH — Confirm tables and condiments wiped/sanitised.
- **Customer toilets checked & stocked** — FOH — Check toilets clean and stocked. Note and fix issues.
- **Allergen requests relayed to kitchen** — FOH — Confirm any customer allergen request was clearly passed to the kitchen. Note the order.
- **Coffee machine cleaned & backflushed** — FOH — Confirm coffee machine cleaned and backflushed.
- **Hot buffet display temperature** — FOH — Probe hot buffet food. Record, photo. Must stay at or above 63°C.
- **Cold buffet display temperature** — FOH — Probe cold buffet food. Record, photo. Must stay at or below 8°C.
- **Buffet out-of-temperature time log** — FOH — Note when food went on display. Cold food out of temperature max 4 hours, then discard.

## SEGMENT 19 — BAR & BEVERAGE
- **Cellar / keg temperature** — Bar — Read and record the cellar/keg temperature. Cask usually 11-13°C.
- **Beer line cleaning** — Bar — Confirm beer lines cleaned (usually every 7 days). Note date.
- **Ice well / scoop hygiene** — Bar — Confirm ice well clean and scoop stored hygienically (not in the ice).
- **Post-mix / soda gun cleaned** — Bar — Confirm soda gun/nozzles cleaned.
- **Glassware condition (no chips)** — Bar — Check glasses for chips/cracks. Remove any damaged.
- **Optics / measures verified** — Bar — Confirm optics and measures are correct (legal measures).
- **Open wine / vermouth dated** — Bar — Confirm open bottles are dated. Discard past their window.

## SEGMENT 20 — HOTEL-SPECIFIC
- **Breakfast buffet temperatures** — FOH — Probe hot and cold buffet items. Record, photo. Hot ≥63°C, cold ≤8°C.
- **Room service tray temp on dispatch** — Chef/Cook — Confirm room service food is at the right temperature when it leaves. Record.
- **Minibar stock & date check** — FOH — Check minibar stock and dates. Note and replace expired items.
- **Banqueting / function hot-hold log** — Chef/Cook — Record hot-hold temperatures for function food. Must stay ≥63°C.
- **Guest allergen request (rooms)** — Management — Confirm guest allergen requests handled and recorded.
- **Poolside / satellite bar hygiene** — Bar — Confirm satellite bar clean and stocked hygienically.

## SEGMENT 21 — MANAGEMENT & COMPLIANCE OVERSIGHT
- **Daily compliance review / sign-off** — Management — Review the day's checks, address any fails, and sign off.
- **Weekly food safety walk-round** — Management — Do a full walk-round. Note and photo any issues.
- **Corrective actions closed out** — Management — Confirm outstanding corrective actions are resolved.
- **SFBB / HACCP diary reviewed** — Management — Review the food-safety diary (4-weekly cycle). Confirm complete.
- **EHO / audit readiness check** — Management — Confirm records and premises are inspection-ready.
- **Staff training records current** — Management — Confirm staff food-hygiene training is current.
- **Supplier approval / due diligence** — Management — Confirm suppliers are approved and due-diligence records held.

## NOTES FOR REVIEW
- **"Everyone" tasks:** handwashing and a few hygiene basics apply to all roles, not just one. I've tagged them to the most likely primary role, but flag if you'd rather they show for every role assigned.
- **Role judgement calls worth a look:** dry-store temp (I put Chef/Cook — could be Porter), delivery checks (Chef/Cook receiving — some venues have a manager do this), equipment deep-cleans (Kitchen Porter (KP) vs Chef).
- **Guidance tone:** written plain and short for a stressed worker. If any read too terse or too wordy, say which.
- Every numeric figure still needs professional sign-off before deployment (unchanged caveat).
