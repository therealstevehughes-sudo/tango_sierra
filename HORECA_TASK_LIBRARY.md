Master content source for the app's task library. Built to be exhaustive across full HoReCa, with every regulatory figure now researched against current UK guidance and sourced. **Content document for review — not yet loaded into the app.**

## Sourcing note (important)
All legal/regulatory figures below are researched against current UK FSA / Food Standards Scotland guidance and legislation, with the basis noted. They are **strong, sourced starting points** — but before real deployment, a qualified food-safety professional must sign them off, because (a) guidance updates, (b) England/Wales/NI and Scotland differ, and (c) some figures are best-practice, not law. Each limit is tagged:
- **[LAW]** — a legal requirement
- **[FSA]** — official FSA guidance (strongly expected by EHOs, not strictly statute)
- **[BEST]** — industry best practice, no legal figure

Key confirmed facts:
- Chilled food: **8°C legal max [LAW]** (Food Safety and Hygiene (England) Regs 2013); **5°C target [FSA]**
- Freezer: **-18°C or below [FSA]** (Reg (EC) 852/2004 requires temp control; -18°C is the FSA standard)
- Hot holding: **63°C or above [LAW]**; below that, 2-hour single time-control window
- Cooking core: **70°C for 2 min, or equivalent e.g. 75°C/30s [FSA]** (Scotland: 75°C/30s for high-risk [LAW])
- Reheating: England/Wales/NI **"steaming hot" ≈70°C/2min [FSA]**; **Scotland 82°C [LAW]** (Food Hygiene (Scotland) Regs 2006)
- Cooling: **below 8°C within 90 min [FSA]** ("90-minute rule")
- Danger zone: **8°C–63°C**; practical EHO limit ~2 hours cumulative
- Fryer oil TPM: **no UK legal limit**; 24–27% is an international best-practice discard benchmark **[BEST]**
